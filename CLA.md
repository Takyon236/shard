# Contributor Licence Agreement

**Shard — Individual and Entity Contributor Licence Agreement, v1.0**

Thank you for contributing. This page is the agreement you accept when you sign
the CLA check on a pull request. It is short on purpose, and this first section
explains why it exists at all before asking you to agree to anything.

## Why we ask for this

Shard is published under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE). There is
also a commercial edition, which is not public. The two share code.

That means a fix you send to a file here may need to ship in a build that is not
under BUSL. Without an explicit grant from you, we could not lawfully do that —
so a one-line fix to a shared module would either be stuck in this repository or
would have to be reimplemented from scratch by somebody who had not read your
patch. Both outcomes are bad, and the second is worse than it sounds.

So we ask for a licence broad enough to cover both editions. We do **not** ask
you to assign your copyright. You keep it. You can relicense your own
contribution to anyone else, including under an open source licence, and nothing
here restricts what you do with your own work.

You sign once. It covers every contribution you make to this project, then and
later.

signed paper agreement instead of the bot flow, say so in your pull request and
we will arrange one — that is a normal request and it will not hold your work up
any longer than it has to.
